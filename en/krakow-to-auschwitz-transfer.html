Good to know

Book your entry first

Entry to the Auschwitz-Birkenau Memorial is booked in advance for a specific time, and the number of visitors in each time slot is limited. Reserve your visit first, then send us the time — we plan the pick-up from your accommodation so that you arrive with a comfortable margin.

The drive

Oświęcim lies about 70 km west of Kraków, and the drive usually takes around 1 to 1.5 hours depending on traffic and where in Kraków you start. The driver collects you at your hotel or apartment and drops you at the Memorial — no changing between trams, trains and buses.

Getting back to Kraków

Most visitors return to Kraków the same day. Tell us roughly when your visit should end and we will include the return trip in your quote. If you are travelling on — for example to Katowice Airport — we can quote that route instead.

A quiet, private ride

A visit to the Memorial is a moving experience. Travelling privately means you set the pace: no group schedule, no crowded coach, and time to talk — or to sit quietly — on the way back.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a transfer from Kraków to Auschwitz?

We quote this route individually, one way or return. Send us your entry time, the number of people and your address and we will reply with a fixed price for the whole vehicle.

How long is the drive from Kraków to Oświęcim?

The route is about 70 km and usually takes 1 to 1.5 hours, depending on traffic and where in Kraków we pick you up.

Do I need to book my entry in advance?

Yes — the number of visitors in each time slot is limited, so it is worth reserving your visit well ahead, before you book the transfer.

Can you collect us after the visit?

Yes — tell us roughly when your visit will end and we will include the ride back to Kraków, or to another address, in the quote.

How do I pay and book?

Fastest on WhatsApp, or use the form on the home page; bookings less than 24 hours before the ride are accepted on WhatsApp only. You pay the driver in cash or by card, with no online prepayment.
