Terminal Change at Pyrzowice — What It Means for Passengers

Katowice Airport in Pyrzowice has been going through a significant reorganisation of its terminal infrastructure, and travellers flying in or out over the coming months should be aware that the usual routines around arrivals, departures, and pick-up points may look a little different than before. Whether you are heading to Krakow, Zakopane, or staying locally, knowing what has changed can save you time and stress right after landing.

What Is Actually Changing

The airport has been expanding and modifying its terminal capacity to handle growing passenger traffic. In practice, this means some flights may be assigned to a different terminal building or pier than in previous seasons, gates can shift, and the walking distance from the aircraft to the arrivals hall may be longer or shorter depending on which section is currently in use. Signage inside the airport is being updated gradually, so it's worth allowing a few extra minutes to find your way, especially if you've flown through Pyrzowice before and are relying on memory rather than checking current signs.

Impact on Meeting Your Driver

For passengers booking an airport transfer, the most practical consequence is the location of the meeting point. Arrivals halls and designated pick-up zones outside the terminal have been adjusted, and the spot where our drivers used to wait may no longer be the closest or most accessible one. To avoid any confusion, we now send each client a short message with the exact pick-up point a day before arrival, along with a photo if the location is new or not obvious. Our drivers also monitor terminal updates directly with the airport, so you don't need to worry about outdated information affecting your pickup.

Why This Matters for Krakow and Zakopane Transfers

If you are being picked up for a transfer to Krakow or Zakopane, a longer walk from a different terminal section or arrivals point can add a few extra minutes to your total journey time. This is usually minor, but if you have a tight connection, a booked mountain excursion, or a hotel check-in deadline in Zakopane, it's worth factoring in a small buffer. Baggage Claim and Parking Adjustments

Baggage claim areas have also been reshuffled in some cases, which can affect how quickly you exit the arrivals hall. If you're travelling with a larger group and using one of our Mercedes Sprinters or a bus, we recommend confirming your exact terminal and gate number as soon as it's available on your airline app, since group logistics benefit from precise timing. Parking and drop-off zones for private vehicles have shifted too, so if you're being dropped off for a departure, double-check the current terminal entrance rather than assuming it matches your last visit.
