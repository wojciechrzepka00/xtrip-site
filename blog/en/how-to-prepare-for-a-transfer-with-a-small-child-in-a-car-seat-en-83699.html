How to Prepare for a Transfer with a Small Child in a Car Seat

Travelling with a baby or toddler through Katowice Pyrzowice Airport and onward to Krakow or Zakopane can be smooth and stress-free, as long as you plan ahead. A private transfer offers far more flexibility than public transport when it comes to car seats, luggage, and unexpected stops, but a little preparation on your part goes a long way toward making the journey comfortable for your child and everyone else in the vehicle.

Book Your Transfer with Child Seats in Mind

When making your reservation, always mention the age, weight, and height of your child so we can prepare the correct type of car seat: infant carrier, convertible seat, or booster. Our fleet, including the Chrysler Pacifica, Mercedes Vito, and Mercedes Sprinter, offers enough space to properly install child seats without cramping other passengers. If you are travelling with more than one child, let us know the number of seats required in advance, as availability can vary depending on the vehicle assigned to your route.

Bring Your Own Seat or Use Ours

Parents often have a strong preference for their own car seat, since children are usually more comfortable and settled in a seat they already know. You are welcome to bring your own and our driver will assist with proper installation. Alternatively, we can provide a suitable seat if you prefer to travel light, especially useful for connecting flights when carrying extra luggage is inconvenient.

Time the Journey Around Your Child's Schedule

The drive from Katowice Pyrzowice Airport to Krakow takes roughly an hour, while the trip to Zakopane is longer, closer to two and a half hours depending on traffic and weather conditions in the mountains. If possible, schedule your transfer around your child's nap time so that part of the journey passes while they sleep. For overnight flights, an early morning transfer often works well since babies tend to doze off in the car after a long flight.

Pack a Small Comfort Kit

Keep a compact bag within reach that includes a favorite toy, a pacifier, a light blanket, spare clothes, and snacks appropriate for your child's age. Wet wipes and a changing pad are useful too, since our drivers are happy to make a brief stop if needed, particularly on the longer route to Zakopane where service stations are available along the way.

Dress Your Child for the Car Seat

Bulky winter coats can interfere with the proper fit of car seat straps, especially during colder months. It's safer to dress your child in thinner layers and cover them with a blanket once they are securely buckled in, rather than strapping them in while wearing a thick puffer jacket.

Communicate Any Special Needs

If your child has motion sickness, allergies, or requires frequent breaks, simply tell us when booking or let the driver know at pickup. Our team is used to travelling with families and will gladly adjust the pace of the journey, make stops, or suggest the smoothest route to keep your little one as comfortable as possible.

With the right preparation, a transfer with a small child can be just as relaxing as travelling without one. Our drivers are experienced with families and equipped to make the journey from Katowice Pyrzowice Airport to Krakow or Zakopane safe, comfortable, and hassle-free for parents and children alike.
