Katowice or Krakow Airport for Zakopane: Which to Pick

2026-11-17

If you are flying to the Tatra Mountains, you will likely land at either Katowice-Pyrzowice Airport (KTW) or Krakow-Balice Airport (KRK). Both are used daily by travelers heading to Zakopane, but they are not identical when it comes to distance, traffic, and the overall journey. Here is a practical comparison to help you decide, or simply to understand what to expect if your flight already brought you to one of them.

Distance to Zakopane

Krakow-Balice is the closer of the two airports to Zakopane. The drive typically takes less time than from Katowice, mostly because Balice sits on the southern side of Krakow, already pointed in the direction of the mountains.

Katowice-Pyrzowice is farther north, so the ride to Zakopane is longer. It is still a very common route, and many visitors choose Katowice because it is served by low-cost carriers with convenient connections from the UK, Ireland, and other parts of Europe.

Which airport has more flights

Krakow Airport generally has a larger schedule of international flights and more airlines operating there. Katowice is smaller but still well connected, particularly with budget airlines.

In practice, many travelers pick whichever airport has the best fare or the most convenient schedule from their home city, then arrange transport to Zakopane afterward. Both airports are realistic starting points for a mountain holiday.

Transfer time and comfort

From Krakow-Balice, the drive to Zakopane is shorter, but it still depends heavily on the time of day, season, and weather. Summer weekends and winter snow can both add significant time.

From Katowice-Pyrzowice, the drive is longer overall, but the road conditions are comparable once you are past the Krakow area and heading south toward the mountains. A private transfer with an experienced driver is the easiest way to manage this longer distance comfortably, especially after a flight.

Regardless of which airport you land at, a private transfer avoids the uncertainty of public transport schedules, luggage handling on multiple buses, or waiting for a shared shuttle to fill up.

Which airport should you choose

If your main priority is the shortest possible drive to Zakopane, Krakow-Balice is usually the better choice. If you find a better flight deal or more convenient connection through Katowice, it is still a perfectly good option — the extra driving time is manageable, particularly with a direct private transfer rather than public transport.

Many travelers also combine the two airports into their trip: flying into one and out of the other, depending on schedules and prices. This is common and works well as long as you plan your transfers accordingly.

Flight schedules and airport details change

Terminal layouts, arrival procedures, and flight schedules can change without much notice. Always check the official website of Katowice Airport or Krakow Airport before your trip for the latest information on terminals and arrivals.
