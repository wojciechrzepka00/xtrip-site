Corporate Transfers and GDPR: How We Protect Passenger Data

2026-11-26

When you book a corporate transfer from Katowice-Pyrzowice Airport (KTW) to Krakow or Zakopane, you share personal details with us: a name, a phone number, flight information, sometimes a company name or billing data. For business travelers and HR departments organizing transport for employees or guests, a natural question arises: how is this information handled, and does it comply with GDPR?

What data we actually collect

Our booking process is designed to ask for only what is necessary to deliver the service. This typically includes the passenger's name, contact phone number, pickup or drop-off address, flight number, and travel date and time. If a company is paying for the transfer, we may also need billing details such as an invoice address or tax ID.

We do not ask for data that has no operational purpose. There is no reason for us to collect information unrelated to organizing a pickup, confirming a driver, or issuing an invoice.

Why the driver needs your details

The driver waiting in the arrivals hall with a name sign needs your name and flight number to identify you and track whether your flight is delayed. This is the minimum information required to make the pickup work smoothly, especially for passengers unfamiliar with the airport or arriving late at night.

This data is shared only with the driver assigned to your transfer and is used strictly for coordinating the pickup, not for any other purpose.

How long we keep passenger data

Booking details are retained only as long as necessary: to complete the transfer, handle any follow-up questions, and meet accounting or legal requirements, such as invoice retention periods required by law. After that period, data is not kept indefinitely without reason.

If your company books multiple transfers over time, we may retain basic contact and billing information to simplify future reservations, but this is based on your consent or an existing business relationship, not an open-ended default.

Your rights as a passenger

Under GDPR, every passenger has the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about them
  • Request corrections if information is inaccurate
  • Request deletion of their data when it is no longer needed for the service
  • Object to specific uses of their data, such as marketing communication

If you want to exercise any of these rights, simply contact us directly through the booking channel you used or via our website. We respond to such requests as part of normal customer service.

Data security in practice

We limit access to booking information to the people who actually need it to organize your transfer, typically dispatch staff and the assigned driver. We do not sell passenger data to third parties, and we do not use it for purposes unrelated to transport services.

If you book through a third-party platform or a corporate travel agent, that organization has its own privacy policy governing how your data reaches us. We recommend reviewing their terms as well, since they control the initial collection point.

What this means for corporate clients

Companies organizing transfers for employees, clients, or event guests between Katowice Airport, Krakow, and Zakopane can treat our booking process as a standard data processing arrangement: we collect what is needed, use it only for the transfer, and do not retain it longer than necessary.

If your company requires a formal data processing agreement for internal compliance purposes, this can be arranged on request before booking a larger group transfer or a recurring corporate arrangement.

Questions about your data

Privacy rules and specific procedures can be updated over time. For the most current details on how your personal data is processed, including any changes related to airport regulations or booking systems, please check our website directly or contact us with specific questions before making a reservation.

Booking a transfer should be simple and worry-free, both in terms of getting from the airport to your destination and in terms of how your personal information is handled along the way.
