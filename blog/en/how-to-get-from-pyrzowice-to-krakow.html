How to get from Pyrzowice Airport to Kraków — all your options

There are several ways to get from Pyrzowice Airport to Kraków: a scheduled bus into the city centre, a train after reaching the nearest station, or a private transfer straight to your address. Each has its own trade-offs.

A private transfer is the most convenient option when travelling with luggage, children, or when you simply want to be dropped off right at your hotel or apartment door, with no transfers and no searching for bus stops.
