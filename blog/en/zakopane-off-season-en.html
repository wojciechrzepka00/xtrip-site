Zakopane off-season — why spring and autumn are worth visiting

Spring and autumn in Zakopane mean smaller crowds, lower accommodation prices, and still-beautiful views of the Tatra mountains — a good alternative for those who prefer to avoid the summer and winter peak season.

It's still worth checking conditions on the access road during these periods — mountain weather can be changeable and may affect travel time.
