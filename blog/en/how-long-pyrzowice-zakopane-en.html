How long does the drive from Pyrzowice to Zakopane take?

The route from Pyrzowice Airport to Zakopane covers about 180 km and typically takes around two and a half hours. In summer, on weekends and long holidays, travel time can increase due to heavy tourist traffic heading toward Zakopane.

In winter, conditions on the route can be more difficult — snow and icy patches in the foothill region can extend the journey further, so it's worth planning a time buffer, especially if you need to arrive at a specific hour.
