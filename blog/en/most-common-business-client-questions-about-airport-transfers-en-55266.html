Most Common Business Client Questions About Airport Transfers

When it comes to corporate travel, reliability and professionalism matter just as much as comfort. At Katowice Pyrzowice Airport Transfers, we work with business travelers every day who need smooth, predictable transportation between Katowice Airport and destinations such as Kraków and Zakopane. Over time, we've noticed that certain questions come up again and again. Here are the answers to the most frequent ones.

How do you handle flight delays?

Business travelers often worry about missed connections due to delayed flights. Our drivers monitor real-time flight status for every booking, so if your plane lands late, your driver will already know and adjust accordingly. You won't be charged extra for reasonable delays, and there's no need to call us in a panic — we're already tracking your arrival.

Can I book a vehicle that allows me to work during the ride?

Absolutely. Many corporate clients choose our Mercedes Vito or Chrysler Pacifica for the extra legroom, quiet cabin, and stable Wi-Fi connectivity, which make it easy to join a call or finish a presentation on the way to a meeting in Kraków. For larger teams traveling together, our Mercedes Sprinter offers more space while still maintaining a professional, comfortable environment.

Do you provide invoices for corporate accounting?

Yes. We issue proper VAT invoices for every transfer, and we can adjust billing details to match your company's requirements, including cost centers or project references if needed. This is one of the most common requests from businesses that book transfers regularly, and we've streamlined the process to make it as simple as possible.

What if my meeting schedule changes at the last minute?

Flexibility is key for business travel. We understand that meetings can run long or get moved up, which is why we allow changes to pickup times with reasonable notice. If you need to adjust your return transfer from Zakopane or Kraków back to the airport, our dispatch team can usually accommodate the change without complications.

How do you ensure driver professionalism?

Our drivers are trained specifically for corporate clients — they dress appropriately, keep conversation minimal unless requested, and prioritize punctuality above all. Many business travelers tell us they appreciate not having to explain their needs twice; our team already understands what a corporate transfer requires.

Can you manage transfers for groups or events?

Yes, this is one of our specialties. For corporate retreats, conferences, or multi-person business trips, we coordinate multiple vehicles — from Mercedes Vito vans to full-size buses — ensuring everyone arrives together and on time. We also assign a single point of contact for group bookings, so you don't have to manage multiple drivers or vehicles separately.

Is it possible to book recurring transfers?

Many of our corporate clients travel to the region regularly for business, and we offer streamlined rebooking for recurring routes between Katowice Airport, Kraków, and Zakopane. Once your preferences are on file — vehicle type, preferred driver, invoicing details — future bookings become much faster.

What happens if my flight is diverted or canceled?

In rare cases of cancellations or diversions, our team stays in contact with you to adjust the pickup location or reschedule the transfer entirely, at no additional inconvenience to you. We understand that in business travel, plans change, and our goal is to make the transportation part of your trip the least stressful element.

If you have a question that isn't covered here, our team is available to discuss your specific corporate travel needs and help you choose the right vehicle and service level for your next trip through Katowice Pyrzowice Airport.
