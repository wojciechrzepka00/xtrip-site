What to Pack for a Weekend in Zakopane

Zakopane, Poland's beloved mountain resort town nestled at the foot of the Tatra Mountains, is a fantastic weekend getaway no matter the season. Whether you're arriving via a private transfer from Katowice Pyrzowice Airport or making your own way south, packing smart will make your short trip far more enjoyable. Here's a practical guide to help you prepare.

Layered Clothing Is Key

Mountain weather in Zakopane is famously unpredictable, and temperatures can shift dramatically between morning and evening, or between the town center and higher elevations if you plan on hiking. Pack a base layer (thermal or moisture-wicking), a mid-layer such as a fleece or wool sweater, and a waterproof, windproof outer jacket. Even in summer, evenings can get chilly, so don't leave a warm layer behind.

Footwear for Every Occasion

Comfortable, sturdy footwear is essential. If you're planning to hike in the Tatras or visit places like Morskie Oko or Gubałówka, waterproof hiking boots with good ankle support are a must. For strolling around Krupówki Street or dining out in the evening, bring a more casual pair of shoes as well. In winter, waterproof snow boots with good grip are non-negotiable, as sidewalks can get icy.

Season-Specific Essentials

If you're visiting in winter, pack gloves, a warm hat, a scarf, and thermal socks. Skiers and snowboarders should bring their own goggles and thermal base layers, even if renting equipment locally. In summer, pack a light rain jacket, a hat for sun protection, sunglasses, and sunscreen — the mountain sun can be surprisingly strong at altitude. A reusable water bottle is handy year-round, especially if you're hiking.

Practical Extras

A small daypack is invaluable for carrying snacks, water, and extra layers during day trips into the mountains. Bring a portable phone charger, as service can be patchy in remote areas and you'll want your camera ready for the scenery. Cash in Polish złoty is useful, since some smaller mountain huts (schroniska) and local vendors may not accept cards. If you plan on visiting a thermal bath, such as those in nearby Bukovina, pack a swimsuit and flip-flops.

Comfort for the Journey

Since your trip likely begins with a transfer from Katowice Pyrzowice Airport, keep your travel documents, phone, and any medications in an easily accessible bag rather than buried in checked luggage. The drive to Zakopane takes a couple of hours through scenic countryside, so it's worth having a neck pillow or light snack on hand, particularly if you're traveling with children. Whether you've booked a Chrysler Pacifica, a Mercedes Vito, or a larger Sprinter or coach for a group trip, having your essentials within reach makes the ride more comfortable.

Don't Forget the Basics

Toiletries, a basic first-aid kit, any personal medications, and a change of clothes in your carry-on are always smart choices in case of delays. If you're visiting during a festival or planning a nicer dinner in town, pack one slightly dressier outfit as well.

With the right gear packed, you'll be ready to make the most of everything Zakopane has to offer — from mountain trails and ski slopes to cozy regional cuisine and relaxing thermal waters. Safe travels, and enjoy your weekend in the Tatras!
