Thermal Pools Under the Tatras: Chochołów, Bukowina or Szaflary for a Day Trip

2026-11-08

If you are heading to Zakopane or the surrounding area, chances are someone has already told you to visit the thermal pools. The Podhale region has several options, and the three most popular are Chochołów, Bukowina Tatrzańska and Szaflary. Each one has a slightly different character, so picking the right one depends on what kind of day you are after.

Chochołów Thermal Baths

Chochołów is often recommended for families and for travelers who want a calmer, more relaxed atmosphere. The complex is known for its wooden architecture that blends with the traditional style of the village, and for pools with varying temperatures spread across indoor and outdoor zones.

It tends to be a good choice if you are traveling with children, since there are usually dedicated shallow areas and slides. It is also a convenient stop if you are driving between Krakow and Zakopane, as it sits just off the main route.

Bukowina Tatrzańska Thermal Baths (Terma Bukovina)

Bukowina is the largest and most resort-like of the three. It has an extensive pool complex, saunas, and views toward the Tatra mountains that many visitors consider the best of the region. This makes it a popular pick for a full-day relaxation trip rather than a quick stop.

Because it is larger and better known internationally, Bukowina can get busy, especially on weekends and during school holidays. If you want fewer crowds, consider visiting on a weekday or arriving right after opening.

Szaflary Thermal Baths

Szaflary is generally the smallest and quieter option of the three, which some travelers prefer if they want a more low-key soak without the resort feel. It is also conveniently located close to the road connecting Nowy Targ and Zakopane.

If your main goal is simply to relax for a couple of hours without planning a full day around it, Szaflary can be an easy, less crowded choice.

Which One Should You Choose?

There is no single correct answer — it depends on your priorities:

  • Traveling with kids and want a family-friendly layout — Chochołów is a safe bet.
  • Want the biggest complex, mountain views and a full spa-like day — Bukowina Tatrzańska fits best.
  • Prefer something smaller, quieter and quick to reach — Szaflary is convenient.

Opening hours, ticket prices and current facilities can change from season to season, so it is always worth checking the official website of the chosen thermal baths before you go, especially if you are planning around a specific schedule.

Final Thoughts

All three thermal baths are worth a visit, and many travelers end up trying more than one during a longer stay in the region.
